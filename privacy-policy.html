Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 2026

Introduction

Liber ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Liber mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of your information when you use our App.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Book Data: Information about books you track, including titles, authors, ratings, and personal notes.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use the App, including reading history, timestamps, and feature usage.
  • Device Information: Basic device identifiers and platform information (iOS or Android).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and improve the App's functionality
  • Personalize your experience and save your reading data
  • Fix bugs and troubleshoot technical issues
  • Understand usage patterns to enhance features

Data Storage & Security

Your book data is stored locally on your device by default. We take reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit when data is synchronized. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is your own. We do not share your reading data with advertisers, analytics companies, or any external services without your explicit consent.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting. These services operate under their own privacy policies and do not receive access to your book library or personal notes.

Children's Privacy

Liber is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please reach out to us through the App's support channel or contact us directly.
